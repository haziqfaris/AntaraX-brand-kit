> # Put Intelligence to Work.

This is the canonical market-facing guide for the AntaraX website. Use it for landing-page structure, product hierarchy, copy, visual hierarchy, SEO/AEO, conversion and future site changes.

## 1. Positioning

**Working category:** AI Products for Business.

AntaraX is a product company, not a consultancy, bespoke software developer, generic AI agency or open-ended R&D firm.

Working explanation:

> **Understand what you have. Run your operations. Make better decisions. Automate what comes next.**

REKA and AntaraX share history and know-how, not the same market position.

## 2. Strategic role

REKA: R&D + innovation + strategic implementation.

When capability becomes proven and repeatable:

AntaraX: products + recurring intelligence + scalable adoption.

REKA explores, researches, prototypes, engineers and validates. AntaraX productizes, deploys, operates, improves and scales.

## 3. Origin

AntaraX must not look like a company created because generative AI became fashionable.

> **We didn't discover AI yesterday.**

AntaraX grew from REKA's years of AI and multidisciplinary R&D experience. Repeatable capability became product.

Use this as heritage and credibility, not as a reason to make AntaraX look like REKA consulting.

## 4. Hard commercial boundary

If a product already solves most of the problem, use the product.

Example: inventory forecasting → Janka.

A novel, multidisciplinary or frontier R&D challenge → REKA.

AntaraX should not say: “Whatever your technology challenge, we'll build something bespoke.”

Instead:

> **Here's the problem. We built a product for it.**

## 5. Product principles

Every product should have:
- a clear problem;
- a clear outcome;
- repeatable value;
- a fast path to value;
- product learning;
- recurring relevance.

Optimize for activation, adoption, retention and expansion — not merely lead generation.

## 7. Product hierarchy

Working model:

**AntaraX → Capability/Technology Family → Customer Product → Product Line → Feature**

Public communication should prioritize the product the customer can actually use.

> **Product clarity first. Ecosystem power second.**

Products appear in two tiers (owner decision, 10 October 2026, replacing the v0.4 tiers):
- **Main products:** Yara (lead), Janka, PLExyz Grid. These get the homepage, the navigation and published pricing.
- **Closed pilots:** Frame (while the owner validates it), PLExyz Crowd, Angka, Tinara, Loom, Motion. They are shown on one Closed pilots page. Each has a "Request pilot access" CTA instead of a waitlist, and an alternative path through REKA's **Managed AI** service.
- **Event organisers:** for PLExyz Crowd and event technology, AntaraX works with **Mole** (mole.is / sense.mole.is). Say this wherever events come up.
- **One form:** demo, pilot and Managed AI requests all go through the same evaluation form. A CTA preselects the intent and product (`/contact?intent=pilot&product=frame`), and the email records the page the visitor came from.
- **Company pages live on reka.re:** About, story, careers and legal are hosted by REKA. AntaraX's own site covers products, solutions, industries, technology, pricing, families and booking a demo.

## 8. Frame

**Digital Infrastructure Intelligence**

> **Know what your business depends on.**

Frame helps organizations understand systems, assets, costs, relationships, renewals and health behind digital operations.

Working lines:
- **Atlas:** inventory/discovery/location/topology
- **Layers:** stacks/dependencies/relationships/ownership/architecture
- **Orbit:** renewals/subscriptions/contracts/expiry
- **Pulse:** health/status/incidents/anomalies
- **Ledger:** costs/vendors/budgets/allocation/forecasting
- **Lens:** AI risks/opportunities/recommendations/analysis

Rule: **Frame is one world with six ways of understanding it — not six separate products.**

## 9. Yara

**Work & Operations**

> **Run everyday work without the chaos.**

Yara is customer-facing. Ripple is better treated as underlying AntaraX capability/family.

Yara is AntaraX's lead product and its first subscription success. It appears first in every product list. Do not state subscriber counts, customers, retention or quotes until the owner supplies them.

Working endorsement: **Powered by AntaraX Ripple**

Working lines:
- **Work:** projects/tasks/jobs/job cards/Kanban/Gantt
- **Desk:** client portal/tickets/maintenance/SLA/AI preprocessing
- **Crew:** teams/workers/assignments/workload/field teams
- **Flow:** rules/approvals/notifications/recurring work/AI automation
- **View:** dashboards/reporting/presentation/team/project views
- **Assist:** chat/summaries/recommendations/ticket analysis/insights

View and Assist remain working names.

## 10. Janka

**AI Inventory Intelligence**

> **Know what you'll need before you run out.**

Janka is inventory intelligence and forecasting. It is not accounting/invoice software.

Working areas: Inventory, Forecast, Replenish, Risk, Insights.

## 11. Angka

*Quieter tier (standalone, not emphasised). Currently a closed pilot: the CTA is "Request an invite". Canonical page: `antarax.ai/angka`.*

**Financial Intelligence**

> **Understand where your money goes.**

Working areas:
- Capture — invoices, receipts, expenses, statements, ingestion
- Match — transactions, evidence, categorization, reconciliation
- Books — P&L, balance sheet, management accounts, COGS/Opex
- Forecast — cash projections, recurring costs, expected income/expenses
- Assist — missing documents, suggestions, certainty, auditor preparation

## 12. Edge / Motion / Loom / Tide

**Edge — Perceive:** sensing, IoT, spatial/location data, environmental monitoring, real-time events, physical-world intelligence. Edge is PLExyz Grid and PLExyz Crowd today.

**Motion — Navigate:** telematics and fleet management for vehicles, built first for autonomous vehicles and remote control. It carries forward the work REKA began with its 2016 self-driving car. Not yet a product: waitlist only, shown like Loom. Make no claims of deployments, customers, pricing or certification until they exist. Robotics research stays with REKA's Kinetik.

**Tide — Optimize:** turning numbers into the next step. Janka forecasts stock, Angka shows where money goes, Tinara finds the one clear move. Janka leads the family; Angka (closed pilot) and Tinara (beta) keep their maturity labels.

**Loom — Orchestrate:** AI agents, knowledge, workflows, automation, integrations, data and digital experiences. Loom is the single automation name. Not yet a product: waitlist only. If Lapisan Automations ships, it is Loom's API surface.

Public packaging remains open. Do not overstate maturity.

## 13. Visual theme

# Earth × Cosmos × Quantum × People

This is visual grammar, not customer taxonomy.

- Earth = real businesses, infrastructure, inventory, physical systems.
- Cosmos = connections, networks, scale, relationships.
- Quantum = intelligence, computation, prediction, possibility.
- People = purpose, decisions, teams, outcomes.

Customer-facing language stays simpler:
**Understand → Operate → Optimize → Perceive → Navigate → Act → Orchestrate.**

## 14. Visual direction

AntaraX should feel simple, friendly, useful, polished, minimal, product-led, 2D-first and easy to scan.

Prefer:
- AntaraX blue;
- Poppins;
- restrained gradients;
- rounded cards;
- approachable iconography;
- real product interfaces;
- subtle thematic cues;
- airy layouts and clear typography.

Avoid:
- heavy sci-fi;
- cinematic space imagery;
- conceptual overload;
- generic AI imagery;
- making visitors decode the theme;
- mascot-led branding unless strategy is explicitly reopened.

## 15. Tone

AntaraX should sound simpler than REKA.

Prefer:
- **Know what your business depends on.**
- **Run everyday work without the chaos.**
- **Know what you'll need before you run out.**
- **Understand where your money goes.**
- **AI that actually helps you get things done.**

Translate jargon into customer language.

## 24. Implementation rule

When changing the site:
1. Preserve the positioning above unless explicitly asked to change strategy.
2. Pair every product name with a plain-language outcome.
3. Show real UI whenever possible.
4. Hide internal taxonomy unless it helps the customer decide.
5. Do not invent customers, usage numbers, integrations, security claims, metrics or maturity.
6. Match CTAs to actual product maturity.
7. Keep the homepage simple and product-led.
8. Treat conversion, SEO/AEO/GEO, accessibility, responsiveness and performance as product requirements.
9. Preserve existing working functionality unless the task requires changing it.
10. Before major copy/IA changes, check this file again.
11. If implementation constraints conflict with this strategy, surface the conflict rather than silently changing positioning.

## 25. Retired names

Do not use these in new copy: "AX Loom" meaning synthetic data, "AX Edge" meaning fleet updates, "AX Motion" meaning workflow automation (Motion now means telematics and fleet management), "AX Kinetics" (REKA Kinetik owns it), and "Yara Ripple" as a product name (use "Yara", with "Powered by AntaraX Ripple" where needed).

## 26. Technology page

Call it **Technology**, not "Lab": lab and research language belongs to REKA. It explains the AI inside each product, the families, Lapisan, security and integrations. One short "From REKA's research" section, labelled experimental, links to REKA.

## 27. Numbers and calculators

Page copy uses industry benchmarks from `BENCHMARKS.md` as percentages, ranges, hours or ratios, each with a named source. Money appears only inside calculators driven by the visitor's own inputs. Calculators show USD first with a flagged toggle to MYR, state the reference rate, and show ranges where the benchmark is a range. Never promise outcomes.

## 28. Industries

Solutions by industry: **Manufacturing, Logistics and 3PL, Ports and terminals, Facilities and property, Retail and venues.** Each page leads with benchmark stats and a value calculator, then where value leaks, how the AI works there, the products involved, how to start small, FAQ and sources.

## 29. Visual motif: BaTech

BaTech (batik teknologi) is the AntaraX corak: Malaysian batik motifs rebuilt from technology, used as backgrounds, fills, cloths and texture. Every motif reads as technology first: traces, pads, chips, lenses, rotors.

- **Malaysian, not Javanese.** Lead with Malaysian motifs: bunga raya, bunga telang, ketam guri, bunga tanjung, padi, pucuk rebung, awan larat, wau bulan, congkak, kerawang. Kawung and parang are Javanese court motifs: never a signature. Research: `docs/research/BATIK_MALAYSIA.md`.
- **No faces in the corak,** except Seth Linton's batik cameo (REKA's character), hidden one at a time. The REKA mark may appear as the maker's signature.
- **Each family has its own motifs and cloth.** The site's shared weave interlocks the pieces like a puzzle; family cloths use mirrored medallions and flowing bands.
- System, rules and code: `docs/design/BATECH.md`, `docs/design/batech-guidelines.html`.
