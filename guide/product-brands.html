Brand architecture

The products came first. AntaraX signs them.

Most AntaraX products are older than the AntaraX name, and people know REKA better than they know AntaraX. So the products keep their own names and logos, take on one shared AntaraX finish, and carry “by AntaraX”. This page shows how, product by product.

Restyling an own mark

Keep the shape. Change the finish. Decided

Nobody should be able to say the logo changed, only that it looks sharper next to AntaraX.

  1. Keep the silhouette and the idea. Do not redraw the geometry.
  2. Highlights come from the sparkle, the five colours inside the A. They are the visible bloodline.
  3. One ink: AntaraX navy, or the product’s own deep colour where the mark is built on it.
  4. Flat fills. No gradients, glows, shadows or bevels.
  5. Never REKA’s colours. REKA orange and REKA blue stay with REKA.
  6. Wordmark in Poppins 600, proper casing, one colour. No multicoloured letters.
  7. Three variants: full colour, white, and one colour.
