## Visual motif base: BaTech (10 October 2026)

The owner chose **BaTech** (Batik teknologi) as the base motif language, replacing the earlier flower and cosmic-bloom motifs. It is a corak: classic Malay batik motifs rebuilt from technology (kawung of chips, parang of data cable, pucuk rebung of circuitry, bunga cengkih as a fan, a circuit-board leaf, an antenna star, a waveform tumpal), used as backgrounds, borders, fills and brand texture. The Earth × Cosmos × Quantum × People grammar (§13) now lives inside the motifs rather than as separate illustrations. Each product family takes its own niche corak from the same set. Full system and rules: `docs/design/BATECH.md`.

### What this deviates from

§12 sets a graduation test for Motion: a repeatable deployment, a published starting price and a fast path to first value. The owner has chosen to list Motion as a waitlist family before that test is met. That matches how Loom is shown: waitlist only, with no claims of maturity, customers or pricing. v0.5 replaces the §12 graduation rule for Motion with a waitlist-only rule. Robotics research stays with REKA's Kinetik.
