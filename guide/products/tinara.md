Own-mark product in the Tide family. Closed pilot, invite-only. Malaysia-first B2B intelligence: which companies to sell to, why, and how to open the conversation.

## Mark

- **Tinara has no logo today**, only a plum square with a white "T" drawn in code (`components/Layout.tsx:63-65`). There is nothing to restyle, so a mark has to be drawn.
- **Brief for the mark:** built from the **kerawang** motif (a lattice with one path through it, 06), drawn to 12's rules: flat fills, navy or Tide-dark ink, highlights from the sparkle palette, readable at 24px.
- **Until it exists:** a wordmark lockup, "Tinara" in Poppins 600 navy with "by AntaraX" beneath. No placeholder letter tile.
