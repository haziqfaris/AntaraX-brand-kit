# AntaraX Industry Benchmarks

**Status:** Approved by the owner on 9 October 2026 as the base for solution, industry and product pages.
**Owner rule:** Page copy uses these benchmarks as percentages, ranges, hours or ratios, always with the source named. Money appears only inside calculators, computed from the visitor's own inputs. Calculators show **USD first**, with a flag toggle to **MYR**.

These are industry benchmarks, not AntaraX results. Never present them as what a customer will achieve. Replace or add AntaraX's own numbers only once they are real and approved.

## How to use a benchmark on the site

1. Quote the range, not a single point, unless the source gives a single figure.
2. Name the source and year in a footnote on the same page.
3. Say "industry benchmark" or "studies report", never "you will save".
4. In calculators, show results as a range when the benchmark is a range.
5. Keep money out of headings, body copy and stat strips. Money belongs inside calculators only.
6. Use the strength rating: **High** can lead a page; **Medium** supports a point; anything weaker is not used.

## Currency in calculators

- Default currency: **USD**. Toggle: **MYR**. Both with flags; the choice carries across every calculator on the site.
- Reference rate: **1 USD = RM4.09**, from Bank Negara Malaysia's closing rate of about RM4.087 on 6 October 2026. The calculator states the rate it uses. Update it before launch and at least quarterly.
- Inputs convert at the reference rate when the visitor switches currency, so results stay consistent.

## Claims we checked and do not use

- A flat "1% manual data-entry error rate": no primary source; Panko's lab work shows 0.5–5% depending on the task.
- "Staff spend 50% of the week chasing information": traced only to a vendor blog.
- Vendor percentages such as "digital work orders cut admin 60%" or "cut errors 75%": no published method.
- "36% unused licences" attributed to Zylo 2025: does not match any other report of that index.
