# AntaraX photo brief

Version 1.0, 10 October 2026. Prompts for an image AI (or a photographer's shot list) for every photo slot on antarax.ai. Brand rules behind it: [guide/06 BaTech illustration](../guide/06-batech-illustration.md) (Photography) and [guide/03 Voice and claims](../guide/03-voice-and-claims.md).

## How to use this brief

The live antarax.ai has **43 photo slots across 17 pages**; each currently shows a batik panel until a photo replaces it.

1. Copy the **style block** below once into your image tool (or paste it before each prompt).
2. For each slot, paste its **Prompt** after the style block, and set the size from its **Shape**.
3. Generate 3 to 4 options, pick one, and save it with the **File** name given.
4. Run the checklist at the end, then hand the files back to be wired into the site.

| Shape | Ratio | Export size | Where it sits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wide | 21:9 | 2520 × 1080 px | Full-width band across a section |
| Landscape | 16:9 | 1920 × 1080 px | Main image beside or under text |
| Portrait | 4:5 | 1600 × 2000 px | Tall image beside a landscape one |

Export as JPEG, quality about 82, sRGB. Pages that moved to reka.re (about, careers, customers, partners, onboarding) are left out; they are not on the live site.

## House style

Every photo should look like a calm, candid documentary shot of real work in Malaysia: plants, warehouses, ports, stores and small offices, never a glossy stock or sci-fi image.

**Style block (paste before every prompt):**

> Photorealistic candid documentary photograph, editorial style, shot on a 35mm lens at eye level, natural available light, shallow depth of field, real working environment in Malaysia, Malaysian people of Malay, Chinese and Indian heritage in everyday work clothes (some women in tudung), relaxed and focused, not posing or smiling at the camera, clean but lived-in surroundings, muted natural colours with a subtle blue accent, no readable text, no logos, no brand names, screens slightly blurred, no holograms, no robots, no futuristic effects.

**Colour grade per slot** (the Grade column):

| Grade | Add to the prompt |
| --- | --- |
| Warm | late-afternoon or warm indoor light, soft golden tones |
| Cool | daylight, crisp neutral-to-cool white balance, soft blue tones |
| Green | soft natural daylight, gentle green-teal tones, fresh and calm |

**People and places**

- Malaysian workplaces and faces; mixed ages and roles (operators, storekeepers, supervisors, planners, office managers, founders).
- Real-looking sites: Penang and Klang Valley plants, Shah Alam warehouses, Port Klang yards, Kuala Lumpur offices.
- Safety gear where the site needs it (hi-vis vests, safety shoes, helmets on yards).
- Phones and tablets show a generic, out-of-focus app screen; never a recognisable product.

**Never**

- Logos, brand names, shipping-line names, container numbers or any readable text (image AIs garble it).
- Glowing brains, robots, holograms, circuit overlays or "AI" visual clichés.
- People smiling at the camera, handshakes, thumbs up, staged teamwork.
- Anything that could be read as a real customer: these illustrate the kind of work, not case studies. No generated photo goes on a customer story.
- Faces in the people-counting shots (PLExyz Crowd, retail crowd): shoot from above or behind so nobody is identifiable.

## Checklist and hand-back

Check each photo against this list before it goes live.

- [ ] Matches its caption: someone reading the caption would expect this picture.
- [ ] Looks Malaysian: the people, the clothes and the workplace.
- [ ] No readable text, logos, brand names, container numbers or licence plates; zoom in on signs, boxes, screens and uniforms.
- [ ] Hands, fingers and faces look natural; tools and machines look physically right.
- [ ] Screens are blurred or show only a vague app shape.
- [ ] Crowd and sensor shots show no identifiable face.
- [ ] Right shape and size: 2520 × 1080 (wide), 1920 × 1080 (landscape), 1600 × 2000 (portrait); JPEG under about 400 KB.
- [ ] Saved with the exact file name from the list.

**Hand-back:** put the files in one folder (or a zip) named `antarax-photos`. Each file replaces the batik panel in its slot, in the order the list gives. The captions stay as written; a photo that misses its caption gets a new prompt, not a new caption.
