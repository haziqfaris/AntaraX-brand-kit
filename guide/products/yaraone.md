# YaraOne

**Internal**, Ripple-aligned, not a public product (16). Written as one word since 16 October 2026; never "YARA" or "YARA One" on screen. Layer **L5 Operations** (17): REKA's operating layer for project delivery, client service, commercial status and live reporting, and since 16 October 2026 the home of every AntaraX product's tickets and feedback. Live at `yo.antarax.ai`; its AXLE at `axle.yo.antarax.ai`.

## Brief

*Facts from `haziqfaris/yara-one` (CLAUDE.md, `docs/`). It is multi-tenant; REKA is tenant one.*

**For whom.** REKA's team and REKA's clients: the team runs projects and support in it; clients see their dashboard and their requests.

**What it does.** One source of truth for projects, requests, deals and reports, with a client dashboard and project support. Clients reach it from inside other apps through a **support link** and an embedded panel (docs/23). **TriageNT**, REKA's support framework, runs inside it: the client sees plain answers; the team sees the reading of the code (docs/TRIAGE.md).

**What it is not.** A generic project manager, CRM, ticket system or chatbot (its own brief).

**Claims.** None in public: it is not listed on antarax.ai.

## Name

- **YaraOne**, one word. Not "Yara One", "YARA One" or "Yara" (Yara is the Ripple product).
- TriageNT: REKA's framework (Esoteriks, L6), shown inside YaraOne. Its client-facing assistant takes a name from the approved persona list, always says it is an AI, and is credited to REKA (owner, 11 November 2026). Team tools keep **Assist**.
