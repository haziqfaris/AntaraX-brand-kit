## Mark: kerawang (owner, 11 October 2026)

**What kerawang is.** Ukiran kerawang is the pierced woodcarving set into the walls of Malay houses, above windows and doors: a carved lattice that lets light and air through while screening the view. It is one of the BaTech motifs (06), described there as "one path found in the noise".

**Why it fits Tinara.** The Malaysian market is the lattice: thousands of companies, each one an opening. Tinara looks through it and finds the few that matter: who to call, why, and how to open the conversation. In the mark, **the lattice is the market, and the one lit opening is the next company to call.** In the larger version, the lit path is the route Tinara works out to that company.

Tinara **keeps its own style** (owner direction, 11 October 2026): plum `#5B2B4A` leads, with gold `#D6A84F` for highlights and amethyst `#8B4E87` as a secondary.
