# Reference documents

The documents this brand kit was built from, copied on 10 October 2026. They are history and detail, not instructions: where they disagree with `guide/`, the guide is current.

- `antarax-brand-guidelines.html`: the master logo file (A mark, sparkle, family icons and lockups, splash and thinking animations, proportion explorer). Open in a browser.
- `strategy/WEBSITE_PLAYBOOK.md` (v0.5) and its amendments, `RECONCILIATION.md` (how REKA and AntaraX split the portfolio), `BENCHMARKS.md` (the cited industry figures the site may use), `DIFFERENTIATION_AND_SITE_FIXES.md`.
- `design/BATECH.md`: the BaTech motif system and its history. The visual guideline (`batech-guidelines.html`, about 13MB) and the motif code stay in `haziqfaris/antarax-ai` (`docs/design/`, `prototype/site/src/batech.mjs`).
- `research/BATIK_MALAYSIA.md`: research behind the Malaysian motif choices.
