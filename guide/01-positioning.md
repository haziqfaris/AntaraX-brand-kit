# 01 Positioning

## The promise

> **Put intelligence to work.**

## Category

**AI products for business.** AntaraX is a product company. It is not a consultancy, a bespoke software house, a generic AI agency or an open-ended R&D firm.

Working explanation:

> Understand what you have. Run your operations. Make better decisions. Automate what comes next.

## Who we talk to

Operations, procurement, finance and facilities leads at small and mid-sized businesses in Malaysia and Southeast Asia, most of them **non-technical**. They want a problem gone, not an AI project. Write for the supervisor who has never heard of a model, and put technical depth one layer deeper (the Technology page, docs, Lapisan).

## Commercial boundary

If a product already solves most of the problem, use the product.

> **Here's the problem. We built a product for it.**

Never say "whatever your challenge, we'll build something bespoke". Novel, multidisciplinary or frontier problems go to REKA.

## How AntaraX relates to REKA

REKA's master brand rules are in [haziqfaris/reka-brand-kit](https://github.com/haziqfaris/reka-brand-kit) (`brand-guidelines.html` v1.0). That file predates the October portfolio; its `guide/01-reconciliation.md` lists the differences (for example, it still files PLExyz and Tinara under REKA divisions and describes Loom, Ripple, Frame and Edge as AI-pipeline sub-products).

| | REKA | AntaraX |
|---|---|---|
| Role | R&D, innovation, strategic implementation | Products, recurring intelligence, scalable adoption |
| Verbs | Explore, research, prototype, engineer, validate | Productize, deploy, operate, improve, scale |
| Sells | Perspective, R&D capability, implementation, **Managed AI** | Product subscriptions; closed pilots run through REKA's Managed AI |
| Site | reka.re (About, story, careers, legal for both) | antarax.ai (products, pilots, solutions, industries, technology, pricing, families, demo) |
| Line | AI for the physical world. | Put intelligence to work. |
| Home in REKA | Four R&D divisions: Harmonik, Kinetik, Heuristik, Esoterik | Heuristik (AI-driven applications) |

- **Legal line:** "AntaraX, a product line of REKA Inisiatif Sdn Bhd."
- **Heritage line,** used once per page at most: "Born from years of R&D. AntaraX was spun out of REKA, where we've been building with AI since 2016." Or: "We didn't discover AI yesterday."
- **Graduation rule (from REKA):** a capability moves to AntaraX when it has a repeatable deployment, a published starting price and a fast path to first value. Until then it stays at REKA. Robotics and autonomy research stays with REKA's Kinetik division.
- **Managed AI** is REKA's service: REKA hosts, watches, improves and keeps the AI reliable on one subscription. Every AntaraX closed pilot runs as part of Managed AI (see 02).
- **REKA-only claims:** "Malaysia's first self-driving car on open roads" is REKA's claim. AntaraX may say "the work REKA began with its 2016 self-driving car", not the "first" claim.
- Do not make REKA explain AntaraX products, and do not make AntaraX sell REKA consulting.

## Origin story (AntaraX's version)

AntaraX tells only how its products grew from REKA: the 2016 car, the move into software automation, the ideas behind PLExyz, Yara, Janka and Tinara, and the Lapisan platform. It lives at antarax.ai/story and links to reka.re for the rest of REKA's history.

## Global stance

Malaysia is origin and proof, not a ceiling. Malaysian craft (BaTech) and Malaysian examples are welcome; "Malaysia's leading…" is not the proposition.
