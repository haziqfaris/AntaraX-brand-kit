**AntaraX → family (capability) → product → product line → feature.**

How each product carries its own mark, colour and credit line (own-mark and A-mark products, "by AntaraX") is in [12 Brand architecture](12-brand-architecture.md); each product's details are in [products/](products/README.md).

### Product facts worth knowing

- **Yara:** work orders and job cards. Workers tap Start, Hold or Complete on their phone; managers see live status, working time and full history. Installable on phones, tablets and desktops; worker screens in English and Malay, office screens in English. Lead product and first subscription success: live with its first customer since 30 September 2026 (owner); name the customer only with written permission. Credit: "by AntaraX". Working lines: Work, Desk, Crew, Relay, View, Assist (View and Assist are working names).
- **Janka:** AI inventory forecasting for manufacturers. Reads real consumption, recommends when and how much to reorder, drafts the order for a person to approve. It is not accounting software. Website working names: Inventory, Forecast, Replenish, Risk, Insights (the app's areas are in 14).
- **PLExyz Grid:** shows what is in a warehouse or yard on the site's own layout, tracks cargo from arrival to dispatch, and checks every placement against the site's rules. Free for one facility; bookings, movements and analytics come with paid plans.
- **AX Frame (AXF):** a manual-first digital-asset ledger: every subscription, API, domain and renewal in one list, warnings before a charge. Working lines: **Atlas** (inventory and location), **Layers** (stacks and dependencies), **Orbit** (renewals), **Pulse** (health), **Ledger** (every asset in one grid), **Lens** (AI analysis). Rule: one world, six ways of seeing it, not six products. Frame never auto-discovers assets (see the axframe repo's guardrails).
- **PLExyz Crowd:** a main product from 25 October 2026, still a closed pilot. It counts people per space with Wi-Fi first, cameras only where a project needs them (owner's answers, 23 October 2026, on antarax.ai). Deployed on real sites since 2021, with the organisations named in 10; the new Crowd app is a closed pilot. Claims rules in [products/plexyz.md](products/plexyz.md).
- **Angka:** website working names Capture, Match, Books, Forecast, Assist (the app's areas are in 14). Free while the pilot runs.
- **Tinara:** Malaysia-first B2B intelligence. Ranks companies by fit, signals and match, and writes a brief: the buyer roles to approach, the opening angle, next steps on days 1, 3 and 7.
- **Cebis Loom:** the Loom family's data product, replacing AX Loom (owner, 25 October 2026). It turns the spreadsheets, exports and scans a business already has into clean typed tables, a data dictionary, tokenised personal data, a report and files ready for AI: ETL for corporates who want value from data they already hold. Sold only as a managed service, by arrangement. Synthetic data comes later. Repository `haziqfaris/antarax-cebis-loom`. Automation is not Loom: it runs on Ripple (see 10, 10 October 2026).
- **AwanGan and BumiNyata:** AntaraX's own AI models (owner, 25 October 2026): AwanGan in the cloud, BumiNyata on the customer's own servers with outbound calls locked. Say "our own models"; do not say they were trained from scratch, name the open models underneath, or give sizes or benchmarks. Neither is live with a customer yet: "By arrangement". AwanGan is also AntaraX's internal AI control plane (10, 25 October 2026); that role is not something customers buy. Repositories `haziqfaris/AntaraX-AwanGan` and `haziqfaris/AntaraX-BumiNyata`.
- **AX Motion:** telematics and fleet management, built first for autonomous and remotely controlled vehicles. Carries forward REKA's 2016 self-driving car work. No claims of deployments, customers, pricing or certification until they exist.
- **Lapisan:** the platform under the products, for developers, at lapisan.antarax.ai.

**Family roles and boundaries:**
- **Ripple:** tasks, work orders, service requests and workflow automation. YaraOne is an *internal* Ripple-aligned support system; Jentera is a candidate, not a listed product.
- **Tide:** Janka (inventory), Angka (finance), Tinara (B2B sales). Lapis by Angka is an independently usable companion within Tide, not a renamed Lapisan module.
- **Loom:** Cebis Loom prepares/structures business data; AX Frame organises a digital estate, dependencies, expenses and renewals. They remain separate products and repositories. Loom does not own general-purpose inference or business workflow execution.
- **Edge:** real-world sensing, spatial models, occupancy and site intelligence; PLExyz is the master product line, with Grid and Crowd. Edge does not own autonomous navigation or general-purpose AI chips.
- **Motion:** telematics, fleet/vehicle operations and remote control within verified maturity. Robots, drones and full autonomous navigation are possible future expansion, not claimed current capabilities. REKA Kinetik keeps frontier R&D.
- **Nalar:** AwanGan delivers cloud AI and BumiNyata delivers private/on-premises AI. HelAI is an exploratory potential embedded/silicon offering, not launched. Heuristiks is REKA-owned engineering framework; Lapisan is AntaraX shared API platform, neither is a Nalar product.

**One primary family per product.** Cross-family technology or optional integrations do not change family ownership. Commercial type and maturity are separate fields (main product, managed service, closed pilot, by arrangement, internal, candidate, future); this change does not alter pricing, CTAs, claims or product availability.

## Naming rules

- Write **AntaraX** (capital A, capital X). The wordmark is set lower case ("antarax") in the logo only.
- Products are named on their own: "Yara", not "AntaraX Yara". Families take the prefix (for example AntaraX Ripple or AntaraX Nalar). **AX Frame** retains its established name but belongs to **AntaraX Loom**, not a standalone Frame family.
- Every product is credited **"by AntaraX"** (12), PLExyz included. Never "Powered by AntaraX", never "by REKA".
- Write **AntaraX**, never "AntaraX AI".
- Historical mark variants (including AXF) remain asset names during migration, not names of current families. The six family names are AntaraX Ripple, Tide, Loom, Edge, Motion and Nalar.
- **PLExyz** is always written PLExyz. It is REKA's spatial intelligence platform, built on AntaraX: Grid (flagship), Trace (built inside Janka), and the concepts Flow, Transit, Crowd, Site, Ward and Yard (products/plexyz.md).
- **Lines and areas inside a product** (Atlas in AX Frame, Forecast in Janka) follow the naming rules in 12: one word, the product first outside the product, shared names (Assist, Forecast) mean the same thing everywhere.
- **Never show internal codenames** to customers: AXLE (REKA's admin console), puzzle-ceplok, puzzle-parang and similar.
