# BumiNyata

Nalar family (Reason). **By arrangement**; not installed at any customer yet (version 0.2.0). AntaraX's AI service on an organisation's own machines, with no internet and nothing calling out. Layer **L3 Intelligence** (17).

## Brief

*Facts from `haziqfaris/AntaraX-BumiNyata` (README, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/DEPLOYMENT-GUIDE.md`). Names, the one-liner and the question are in 02 and win over this sheet.*

**For whom.** Organisations that cannot send their documents to anyone's cloud, AntaraX's included.

**The problem.** "How do we use AI and keep every byte on our own ground?"

**What it does.** BumiNyata reads PDFs, scans and photos, finds personal data and pulls the fields out of invoices and receipts, keeping every result sealed per department or customer. It runs on one machine with Docker Compose, CPU only, with an admin console, quotas and an audit log. Nothing calls out: no telemetry, no update checks, no licence server; the licence is a signed file checked on the machine.

**What it is not.** A cloud service (that is AwanGan); a chatbot.

**Claims.**
- May say: "Our own AI models, on your own servers."; that it works with no internet and nothing calls out; that work never stops because a licence ran out.
- Never say: that the models were trained from scratch, which open models sit underneath, sizes or benchmarks (02); installations before there are any.

**How it is sold.** By arrangement ("Talk to us").
