# 12 Brand architecture

How AntaraX, its products and REKA carry their names, marks and colours, and how AntaraX's style reaches into each product without replacing the product's own. Each product's details are in [products/](products/README.md). The visual version is [`guide/product-brands.html`](product-brands.html). When each rule was decided is in [10 Decisions log](10-decisions-log.md); what is still open is in [11 Open questions](11-open-questions.md).

## Why products lead

Most AntaraX products are older than the AntaraX brand, which launched as a product line on 23 July 2026:

- **PLExyz** has been a REKA product with its own logo since at least January 2023;
- **Janka, PLExyz Grid and Tinara** arrived with their own names and identities;
- **Angka** followed in September 2026 with its own mark and canon.

Customers and partners know REKA, and some know these products. So AntaraX does not replace the product brands. It **endorses** them and adds one small shared layer to each.

## The model

```
REKA Inisiatif Sdn Bhd            the company: R&D, Managed AI, legal entity
 └─ AntaraX                       the product line: every product is "by AntaraX"
     ├─ Families                  capability groups on antarax.ai (Ripple, Edge, Frame, Loom, Motion, Tide)
     └─ Products                  the brands customers buy
         ├─ own-mark products     keep their own logo
         └─ A-mark products       use the AntaraX A with their family icon
```

- **The product is the brand the customer buys.** Lead with the product name and its plain outcome (03 Voice).
- **Every product is "by AntaraX".** One credit line, the same everywhere.
- **AntaraX owns the shared layer:** type, text and status colours, interface rules, the BaTech motif language, the AI sparkle, antarax.ai, Lapisan and the API.
- **Families group products** on antarax.ai (the Families and Technology pages, family badges and colours). They are not part of a product's credit line.
- **REKA is the company behind AntaraX:** the legal line, the Managed AI operator, and the "from REKA" trust line.
- **PLExyz** is REKA's spatial intelligence platform (Harmonik division), built on AntaraX. Its products are credited "by AntaraX" like every other product.

### Two kinds of product

| Kind | Products | Mark | Primary action | Accent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Own-mark** | PLExyz products, Janka, Angka, Tinara | The product's own logo | The product's own colour (its dark shade if it fails 4.5:1 with white) | The product's own colours |
| **A-mark** | Yara, AX Frame, Loom, Motion, and every new product | The AntaraX A with the family icon in its centre (04) | The product's own colour (14) | The family colour |

A product is own-mark when it arrived with an identity people already recognise. Everything else starts as A-mark.

## Two layers in every product

**The product layer is the product's own** and stays as it is: logo, palette, signature details, layouts.

| Product | Signature details |
|---|---|
| PLExyz Grid | Force field, shard and zone grid; the four zone colours; the multicoloured "Grid" wordmark; the hex-tile sign-in |
| Janka | The J that never closes; the signal ticks; its splash and thinking animations |
| Angka | The sheet-stack face; paper ground; plum and Lapis peach |
| Tinara | Garnet and gold; the signal logo (14) |
| AX Frame | The breathing cube halo |
| Yara | The A with the Ripple wave; teal |

**The AntaraX layer is the same in every product**, and small enough that it never changes how the product looks:

1. **Credit line:** "by AntaraX" (see below).
2. **BaTech, quietly:** the product's signature motif in empty states, and a Quiet band of its family's corak on dividers, sign-in and email headers, following the BaTech guidelines (see below).
3. **AI sparkle:** the AntaraX sparkle, the one inside the A (`assets/ai/sparkle.svg`, taken from the master mark) beside every piece of AI work: a forecast, a ranking, a drafted order or brief, a suggested match. The same sign in every product tells a customer that this is AntaraX intelligence. Never decoration, never a logo.
4. **Thinking motion:** while AI works for more than a moment, the product's own mark moves the AntaraX way, its separate pieces drifting in and out of step (04, animation 2). Respect reduced motion.
5. **Shared core:** Poppins, JetBrains Mono for every number, the shared status colours, the AntaraX-blue focus ring (see "Colour").
6. **Intan eyebrow:** section eyebrows start with the intan (a 9px diamond) in the product's accent.
7. **App tile:** one tile shape for every product.
8. **Footer:** the legal line, and "Lapisan by AntaraX" wherever the product mentions its API.

### Colour: the family pair, never the product's colours

BaTech's rule is "use the palette and the family pairs; never recolour with off-brand colours". So a product's motif is always drawn in **its family's pair** (colour, dark, ground), not in the product's own colours: Janka's padi is Tide pink, Grid's petak is Edge green. This is also how the motif shows which family a product belongs to. The product's own colours stay in its own interface.

### Where it goes in a product

Each place uses one BaTech strength, keeps text on a solid surface, and appears **once per screen**.

| Place | What | Strength and size |
|---|---|---|
| Empty states | The product's signature motif alone, as the picture | Full colour (family pair), 72px |
| Dividers, email and report headers | The family's BaTech **band** (14), or a drift band that thins out with no edge | Quiet inside the app, Colour on covers |
| Sign-in brand panel, splash, covers | The family's BaTech **panel** (14): its own shape, in Colour on near-white or White on the product's colour; the logo sits clear of it | Colour or White; PLExyz Grid takes only an Edge spot for now |
| Avatar and image placeholders | The family's corak behind initials or an empty image | Quiet or family pair, texture scale |
| Loading | The motif draws on, lights in sequence or carries a travelling pad; or the mark does its thinking motion. Never spinning or flipping (14) | Full colour, 48 to 72px |

Files: `assets/products/<product>/<product>-motif.svg` (signature motif, family pair) and `assets/families/bands/<family>-quiet-drift.svg` and `-quiet-edge.svg` (Quiet bands), built by `tools/build-product-motifs.mjs` with the BaTech guideline recipes.

A logo and a motif are always different (14). Tinara's logo was its motif; it now gets a logo of its own, and kerawang stays its motif.

### Never

- A full cloth or medallion inside a product. Full colour only in the family sets' places (sign-in panels, splash, covers, spots; 14), never behind text or data.
- Text or data on top of full-colour corak (BaTech: never behind paragraphs).
- A motif under 24px on screen (6mm in print); below that, dots only.
- One motif shown large on its own: above about 120px, show at least two motifs so it reads as corak, not a logo.
- A motif recoloured in the product's own colours, stretched or squashed.
- **A straight cut.** BaTech is mixed patterns, never straight lines (06): no hard-edged strip inside content, no ruled line, row of dots or row of identical motifs as decoration. Bands drift (fade out with no edge) or sit against a container's own edge, where BaTech allows a crop.
- Cameos on single motifs (empty states, loaders). Bands carry BaTech's hidden cameos (Seth, the REKA mark, the AntaraX A) as the motif code places them, at most one each per area.
- Motion that is not slow and rare: nothing spins or flips; all motion stops for reduced motion.

## Signature moments

Approved by the owner (11 October 2026). Build Yara's first, then Janka's. Beyond the quiet placements, each product can give its motif **one job**: a moment where the motif does what the product does. Each follows BaTech: the official drawing, the family pair, at least 24px, motion slow and rare (draw on, fill, turn), never behind data, and nothing moves for people who turn motion off.

| Product | Moment | What the motif does |
|---|---|---|
| Yara · awan larat | A job is completed | A short awan larat trace draws from the Complete button to the next stage's card, and a pad travels along it: the work carried on |
| Janka · padi | A forecast runs | The padi stalk draws on while the forecast runs; its grains fill when it is ready |
| Angka · congkak | Month-end matching | Progress is the congkak board: each matched receipt drops a seed into a hole |
| Tinara · kerawang | Ranking companies | The lattice lights opening by opening until one path settles |
| PLExyz Grid · petak | An empty layout | Before a facility is drawn, the canvas shows petak as the picture and asks for the first zone |
| PLExyz Crowd · orbit | Live sensing | The live indicator is the orbit, its dot travelling slowly, in place of a pulsing dot |
| AX Frame · intan | A record fills in | The intan's facets fill as each part of an asset's record is completed |

Across every product:

- **Milestones:** the first job done, the first forecast, the first match. A one-time card with the motif in full colour and the family's Quiet band.
- **User guides and help:** the Quiet band as the chapter divider; the motif on the guide cover.
- **Reports and exports:** the Quiet band across the cover, the motif beside the title.
- **Hardware and signage:** Crowd nodes, Grid bay signs and Trace RFID tags carry the motif (6mm minimum in print).

One moment per product. Never on screens people work through quickly (tables, forms, the board itself).

## Elements, charts, loading and naming

### Elements

**Functional controls stay plain geometry; BaTech is never drawn as a straight line or strip.** Where a control needs a line (a progress bar, a stepper, a gridline), it is a plain line in the product's colours, not corak. BaTech reaches controls only in shapes that are not straight:

| Element | Detail | From BaTech |
|---|---|---|
| Progress bar | A plain bar in the product colour, ending in a round pad (a filled dot with a white ring) | the solder pad at the end of a trace |
| Stepper and timeline | Plain dots and a plain connecting line; done steps filled, the current one ringed | the pad |
| Section divider | A Quiet drift band (fades out with no edge), or simply white space; never a ruled line or row of dots as ornament | the drift band |
| Active tab or nav item | A short awan larat curl under the active item: a curving trace ending in a pad | awan larat |
| List markers and eyebrows | The intan, 9px | intan |
| UI icons | A plain line icon set drawn with BaTech's line rules: one stroke weight (1.75px at 24px), round caps and joins. Icons are not motifs | one outline, round caps |

### Charts

The product's own colours draw the data. BaTech never sits behind or inside a plot. Three details carry the family resemblance:

- **The latest value ends in a pad:** a filled dot with a white ring at the end of a line.
- **Flagged points are an intan** (a diamond marker, at least 8px): an anomaly, a breach, a renewal.
- **AI forecasts** continue the line dashed, with the sparkle beside their label.

Gridlines are plain and recessive (dotted is fine; they are not corak). An empty chart shows the product's empty state. Series identity is never carried by motif shapes; colour plus a label does that.

### Loading

The full loading system (seven states, and each product's own loaders) is in 14. In short:


| Wait | What shows |
|---|---|
| Under a second | Nothing |
| A screen loading | A skeleton in the product's neutrals; no motif over data |
| A task the person started (a forecast, a ranking, an import) | The signature motif as the loader, with the action in words ("Forecasting 48 items") |
| Long AI work | The product's own mark in its thinking motion (04) |

Motifs **draw on**, **light in sequence** or carry a **travelling pad**; marks **breathe** and their pieces drift out of step. Nothing spins or flips. Each product's loaders are in 14.

### Naming lines and areas

Products are named on their own (02). Inside a product, its parts are named in one of two ways, and each product uses **one** of them:

| Kind | What it is | How it is named | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lines** | Distinct ways of using one product, each with its own screen and person | One evocative English word | AX Frame: Atlas, Layers, Orbit, Pulse, Ledger, Lens. Yara: Work, Desk, Crew, Relay, View, Assist |
| **Areas** | Sections of the app | The job, in a plain word | Janka: Inventory, Forecast, Replenish, Risk, Insights. Angka: Capture, Match, Books, Forecast, Assist |
| **Products in a line** | Separate products under one line name | Always the line name first | PLExyz Grid, PLExyz Trace, PLExyz Flow |

How to write them:

- **Inside the product**, the line or area alone: "Atlas", "Forecast".
- **Outside the product** (antarax.ai, sales, docs), the product comes first: "Atlas in AX Frame" in running text; "AX Frame · Atlas" where space is short. Lines are ways of using one product, not products, so never "AX Atlas", "AntaraX Atlas" or "Frame Atlas" as a product name.
- **One word, capitalised as a name.** No all caps, no prefixes, no version numbers in the name.
- **Shared names mean the same thing everywhere.** **Assist** is the AI assistant in every product, always shown with the sparkle. One exception: Angka keeps **Ask Angka**, because a business owner understands "ask" at once (owner, 11 October 2026). **Forecast** is a product's forecasting. A product that adds an AI assistant calls it Assist.
- **Never reuse** a family name (Ripple, Edge, Frame, Loom, Motion, Tide), a product or PLExyz product name, another product's line for a different thing, or an internal name (AXLE).
- **Malay words** may name a line only when the meaning is the job and it is explained once, as Lapis and Lapisan ("layer") are. Motif names (awan larat, padi, intan) stay design vocabulary, not feature names.
- **Working names** stay marked as working until the owner confirms them (Yara View and Assist today).

Name clashes, settled (11 October 2026):

1. **Yara's Flow line is renamed Relay** (passing work on by rules, approvals and reminders), so PLExyz Flow keeps its name.
2. **AX Frame's Lens stays:** it is a view of analysis (risks, opportunities, recommendations), not an assistant.
3. **Orbit** is AX Frame's renewals line and the BaTech motif for PLExyz Crowd. Motif names never reach customers, so this is allowed.
4. **Ask Angka stays** as Angka's assistant name.

BaTech's words are the vocabulary for design and code (component and token names such as `intan`, `pad`, `drift`, `quiet`, `loader-padi`); customers never see them.

## Own marks

Own marks stay as they are. A mark may be refreshed when it has a problem (trace noise, colours that clash with the shared core), keeping its shape:

1. Keep the silhouette and the idea.
2. Highlights from the sparkle colours inside the A: purple `#524FE9`, orange `#F47B3D`, cyan `#0CC4FB`, emerald `#12B76A`, indigo `#7171EA`.
3. One ink: AntaraX navy `#0B1B4F`, or the product's own deep colour.
4. Flat fills: no gradients, glows, shadows or bevels.
5. Wordmark in Poppins in the product's proper casing; a designed wordmark (Janka, PLExyz Grid) stays as designed.
6. Variants: full colour, white, one colour.

Janka's mark follows these rules (navy ink, sparkle orange and cyan).

## Lockups

```
[mark]  Janka                    [mark]
        by AntaraX                Janka
                                by AntaraX
   horizontal                    stacked
```

- **"by AntaraX" for every product,** set as text: Poppins 500, about 45% of the product name's size, in body `#3B4469` (white at 80% on dark). Never put the AntaraX A next to an own mark: one mark per lockup.
- In one line: "Janka · by AntaraX". Never an em dash.
- Clear space: half the mark's height on every side.
- Minimum: mark 24px high (PLExyz Grid 48px for the full-colour mark, its solid variant below that). Under 120px of lockup width, drop "by AntaraX".

### App icon tile

- White tile, corner radius 22% of its width; the mark centred at 62% of the tile width.
- Maskable variant (PWA): the mark at 50%.
- Dark variant: navy `#0B1B4F` tile with the white mark.
- Favicon: the mark alone when it reads at 16px; otherwise the tile.

## Adding a product

1. Start as A-mark in its family (if the family has none yet; otherwise draw its own mark, 14), acting in its own colour.
2. Give it the AntaraX layer: "by AntaraX", a signature motif from its family's niche (drawn by the BaTech rules), the AI sparkle, the shared core.
3. Write its sheet in `guide/products/` and add it to 02 and the motif map.

## Do and don't

- **Do** keep each product's logo, colours and signature details.
- **Do** credit every product "by AntaraX".
- **Do** mark every piece of AI work with the sparkle.
- **Do** follow the BaTech guidelines for every motif: family pairs, three strengths, 24px minimum.
- **Don't** put a batik pattern or cloth inside a product.
- **Don't** put two marks side by side (the product's and the A).
- **Don't** credit a product "by REKA" or "Powered by".
