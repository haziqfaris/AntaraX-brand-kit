# 03 Voice and claims

## Voice

AntaraX sounds **simpler than REKA**: plain, friendly, useful, cut to the chase. Write for a non-technical operations lead who has two minutes.

- **Problem first, product second.** Open with the reader's problem in their words, then the product that removes it.
- **Pair every product name with a plain outcome.** "Yara shows where every job stands, live", never just "Yara".
- **One idea per sentence.** Short paragraphs, one to three sentences.
- **Frame AI as a business change, not a technology.** "AI does the maths. People decide." Say what the AI does in the job (forecasts, ranks, counts, drafts) and that a person approves.
- **Translate jargon.** "Wi-Fi signals already in the room", not "Wi-Fi CSI"; "how connected that work is", not "data maturity".
- **Name the buyer concretely** where it helps: the role, the floor, the size of operation.

Reference lines:

- Put intelligence to work.
- Know what your business depends on.
- Run everyday work without the chaos.
- Know what you'll need before you run out.
- Understand where your money goes.
- AI that actually helps you get things done.

## Style

- **Sentence case** everywhere: headings, buttons, labels, navigation. No all-caps labels (mono labels for data only).
- **British English** (organise, colour, optimise), except product family verb "Optimize" as set in the master logo file.
- **No em dashes** in customer copy. Use a colon, a full stop or "and".
- **Numbers** in JetBrains Mono when they are data (scores, counts, IDs); spelt naturally in prose.
- **Headlines end with a full stop** when they are statements ("Request pilot access."), not when they are questions.
- **Malay words** from the batik vocabulary (corak, kain, awan larat) appear in design documentation, not in customer copy unless explained.

## CTA vocabulary

| Situation | Label |
|---|---|
| Main products | **Book a demo** |
| Closed pilots | **Request pilot access** (button), "Request access" in tight cards |
| Managed AI beyond the pilots | Talk to us about Managed AI |
| Secondary | See the products · See [product] · How the pilot works · See pricing |

Reassurance line under main CTAs (only while true): "About two minutes of questions. The demo starts from your answers, and there is no commitment." Pilots: "A few questions about your work. No commitment; the team replies to say whether the pilot fits."

Rules: one primary CTA per view; the same label everywhere for the same action; the CTA matches the product's real maturity. Add risk reversal near the CTA when true ("about two minutes", "the demo starts from your answers").

## Claims rules (never break)

Shared with REKA (REKA Brand Guidelines v1.0): **"Founder", never CEO**, for Muhammad Haziq Faris in any context; no em dashes; flag illustrative figures "(illustrative)"; "Malaysia's first", never "Southeast Asia's first".


1. **Never invent** customers, logos, quotes, testimonials, usage numbers, subscriber counts, retention, awards, certifications, partnerships, integrations, security claims or timelines.
2. **Proof slots stay empty** until the owner supplies real, permitted proof. Show "Previews use example data" under any mock UI with sample figures; label sample data "Example data".
3. **Numbers in page copy** are published industry benchmarks with a named source (percentages, ranges, hours, ratios). Money appears only inside calculators driven by the visitor's own inputs: USD first, MYR toggle, the reference rate stated, ranges where the benchmark is a range. Never promise outcomes.
4. **Maturity is stated honestly.** Closed pilot means closed pilot. Do not present roadmap as shipping.
5. **PLExyz Crowd:** no accuracy percentages, no named customers or logos, no vital-sign sensing claims, no roadmap shown as shipping; its demo is simulated and must say so; the internal codename AXLE is never shown. Describe the sensing as "Wi-Fi signals already in the building" (Bluetooth may be mentioned where the product does it).
6. **AX Motion:** no claims of deployments, customers, pricing or certification. "Malaysia's first self-driving car on open roads" is REKA's claim only.
7. **Frame:** never imply auto-discovery, network scanning or shadow-IT detection. Frame is manual-first; the user tells it what exists.
8. **REKA recognition:** Frost & Sullivan "recognised" REKA's crowd analytics work; never "award".
9. **Mole:** describe as a collaboration for event technology, nothing more.

## Words to avoid

Revolutionary, cutting-edge, next-gen, unlock, empower, seamless, leverage, synergy, "AI-powered" as a headline on its own, "Oops"/"Uh oh" in errors (state what failed), exclamation marks except for confirmed critical alerts.
