Brand architecture

Keep each product’s style. Add the AntaraX layer.

Most AntaraX products are older than the AntaraX name. So every product keeps its own name, logo, colours and signature details, and every one is “by AntaraX”. AntaraX reaches into each product through the same small touchpoints, among them one quiet motif and one sign for AI.

Two layers in every product

The product stays itself. AntaraX adds a little.

  • PLExyz GridForce field, shard and zone grid; zone colours; the multicolour wordmark; the hex-tile sign-in

The AntaraX layer is added

  1. Credit line“by AntaraX” for every product, and “from REKA” on sign-in and reports.
  2. Product motifOne motif from the product’s family, drawn simply in its colour, used faintly once per screen.
  3. AI sparkleThe four-petal sparkle beside every piece of AI work: a forecast, a ranking, a draft.
  4. Thinking motionWhile AI works, the product’s own mark moves the AntaraX way: pieces drift out of step.
  5. Shared corePoppins, JetBrains Mono for numbers, shared status colours, AntaraX-blue focus ring.
  6. Intan eyebrowSection eyebrows start with the 9px diamond, in the product’s accent.
  7. App tileOne tile shape for every product, so they read as one family on a home screen.
  8. FooterThe legal line, and “Lapisan by AntaraX” wherever the API is mentioned.
The motif map

One motif per product, from its family

How quiet

Once per screen, faint, never a pattern

The logo is always the hero. The motif sits behind it at about 10%, cropped by a corner. In empty states it is small, in the product’s tint. In emails it is a 24px mark in the corner. The full batik cloth stays on antarax.ai.

Tinara

The kerawang window Choose one

What kerawang is

Ukiran kerawang is the pierced woodcarving set into Malay houses above windows and doors: a carved lattice that lets light and air through while screening the view.

Why it fits Tinara

The market is the lattice: thousands of companies, each one an opening. Tinara looks through it and finds the one worth calling, why, and how to open. The lit gold opening is the next company to call.

Window Recommended
Tinaraby AntaraX

An arched Malay window with its carved lattice, the top opening lit. The most specific to kerawang, and unlike any other mark in the portfolio.

Lattice
